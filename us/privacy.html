Private Pilot Prep: FAA Exam for iPhone, iPad and Mac

Privacy Policy

Private Pilot Prep: FAA Exam does not collect any personal information. There is no account and no advertising. The app sends anonymous usage statistics (which features are used, never your answers) unless you switch them off in the Library. Apart from that, the only network traffic is Apple's: if you leave iCloud sync on, your own iCloud account carrying your progress between your devices, and the App Store handling the optional Full Access purchase.

What the app collects

No personal information. Private Pilot Prep: FAA Exam does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal detail, and it contains no advertising and no tracking technologies.

Anonymous usage statistics

To see which parts of the app are used and where people stop, the app sends a small number of anonymous events to TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service that stores its data in the European Union. An event is a short label such as "mock exam finished" or "Full Access screen shown", together with the app version, the kind of device (iPhone, iPad or Mac), the language setting, whether Full Access is unlocked or was just bought, and for a finished session a score band (for example 60 to 79%).

Events never contain your name, email address, Apple Account, questions, answers, or exact scores. Each install is counted with a random identifier created on the device; it is hashed before it leaves the device, is not linked to you or your Apple Account, and is not used to track you across other apps or websites. TelemetryDeck does not store IP addresses.

You can turn this off at any time in Library, "Share anonymous usage statistics". Once it is off, the app records and sends no further events.

What the app stores, and where

The app saves your study data so your progress survives between sessions. This includes:

  • which questions you have answered and whether you answered them correctly,
  • the spaced-review schedule built from those answers,
  • your mock exam results and timings,
  • a mock exam you leave before submitting it, so you can resume it on that device,
  • a count of the free practice questions and free mock exams you have used.

All of it is written to a single file inside the app's own storage on your device — on a Mac, inside the app's sandbox container. It is never sent to any server belonging to the developer, and the developer cannot see it.

iCloud sync

With iCloud sync on (Library › Sync, on unless you turn it off), each of your devices also keeps a copy of its own progress in the private database of your iCloud account, through Apple's CloudKit, so your iPhone, iPad and Mac can combine their progress. The copy includes the count of free practice questions and free mock exams used, so the free allowance is shared by your own devices. Only devices signed in to your Apple Account can read that copy; the developer has no access to it, nothing is stored on any server of ours, and it counts towards your own iCloud storage (a few tens of kilobytes per device). Turn sync off in the Library to keep progress on one device only. To delete the iCloud copy, use Settings › your name › iCloud › Storage › PPL Prep US on an iPhone or iPad.

You can erase your progress at any time with "Reset all progress" in the Library tab — with sync on, on every device signed in to your iCloud account. Deleting the app deletes the copy on that device.

Network access

Apart from the anonymous usage statistics described above, the app contacts no server belonging to the developer. Every question, explanation and study note is bundled inside the app; the practice nav logs, weight and balance sheets, weather reports and calculations are generated on the device; and everything works entirely offline.

The optional Full Access purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit): Apple processes the payment and tells the app on your device whether the purchase exists, and Apple's own sheet checks any offer code you redeem. The developer receives no personal or payment information.

Children

Private Pilot Prep: FAA Exam is a study aid for private pilot certificate candidates and is rated 4+. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
