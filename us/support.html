Private Pilot Prep: US for iOS

Support

A study aid for the FAA Private Pilot - Airplane knowledge test (PAR) — regulations, weather, airspace and airports, navigation, aircraft and systems, performance and weight and balance, and human factors.

Questions, bug reports, or a question you think is wrong? Email patrguzz@gmail.com. Corrections to the question bank are especially welcome — please include the question and the reference you're working from (AIM paragraph, 14 CFR section, or FAA handbook chapter).

Requirements

iPhone or iPad running iOS 27.0 or later. The app works entirely offline; no internet connection is needed at any point.

How the app works

Practise by topic with instant feedback, or sit the full 60-question mock test under the real 2-hour-30-minute time limit with the pass rule as the examiner applies it — 70% overall — or a shorter timed quiz on one subject. Every answer is tracked: missed questions come back on a spaced schedule in Smart Review, and the Home tab estimates your readiness by area and topic. The Nav Log Trainer plans a fresh VFR cross-country between real aerodromes and asks the heading, groundspeed, time, fuel and compass questions computed from it; the Loading Sheet Trainer generates a weight-and-balance problem; the METAR & TAF Trainer generates a weather report and forecast to decode; and the Calculation Drills generate density altitude, time-speed-distance, fuel, time zone, crosswind and similar problems with worked solutions.

Progress and resetting

All progress lives on your device only. To start over, use "Reset all progress" at the bottom of the Library tab. Deleting the app also deletes all progress — there is no cloud copy.

A question looks wrong?

Every question carries an explanation and a source reference. If you believe an answer conflicts with the current AIM, 14 CFR or FAA handbooks, email the question text and your reference — verified corrections ship in the next update. Always treat the current official publications as authoritative.

About the nav-log aerodromes

The airports in the Nav Log Trainer are real United States airports, positioned from public-domain data so that tracks and distances are realistic. The winds, variation, airspeed, fuel flow and deviation card are generated for practice and are labelled as planning variations. Never use a generated log for a real flight.
